Values applied to the identity

Honesty

The basis of client relationships, ensuring every interaction is clear and direct.

Trust

A pillar of any credit company, essential for the market to see Rino as a solid, safe partner.

Ethics

The guide for business conduct, making sure decisions are always fair and responsible.

Symbolism

The rhinoceros as a metaphor for Rino's stance and presence in the market.

Strong

Just as the rhino is one of the largest land animals, Rino positions itself with confidence and determination.

Robust

The animal's imposing build reflects the company's solidity: a firm, stable base built to last in the financial market.

Impactful

The animal shapes the savanna and opens paths for other species. Likewise, Rino transforms its ecosystem and creates opportunities for partners.

Non-predatory

As a herbivore, the rhino does not hunt. This translates Rino's philosophy of growing without aggressive practices.

02 / 08

Logo

The system has a symbol (silhouette), a wordmark and four lockups for different formats.

Symbol

Silhouette inscribed in a 9X by 20X rectangle (20:9 ratio). Four vertices control the proportion: the horn tip, the highest point of the back, the tail curve and the base of the front leg.

RINO

Wordmark

The stem of the “I” sets the stroke module (M). R, N and O keep the same weight; spacing is optically adjusted between 1.0 and 1.5 M. Minimum clear space: 1 module on every side.

Versions: vertical, horizontal, band and reduced
